## Capture guidance
1. Open the running site from the Codespaces Ports panel.
2. Use your browser's screenshot tool or your operating system's screenshot shortcut.
3. Save actual captured images in this folder using clear filenames such as `current-interface.png`, `employee-dashboard.png`, `admin-dashboard.png`, `schedule-management.png`, and `leave-management.png`.
4. Avoid submitting broken preview placeholders or blank images. Open each saved image once to confirm it displays correctly.
